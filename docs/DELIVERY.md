# Delivery gates

1. **Code complete:** local capture, explicit samples, evidence, filters, revisions, and ICS export pass tests.
2. **Integration ready:** a same-origin adapter rejects missing configuration; no provider claim exists without a verified endpoint.
3. **Pilot ready:** a named owner supplies the DigitalOcean Gemma endpoint and credentials through deployment secrets, validates a non-sensitive fixture, and agrees cost/rate limits.
4. **Launch ready:** institutional approval, publishing workflow, privacy review, observability, incident owner, and real notice governance are complete.

Never bypass the gates by substituting a sample as a live provider result. Do not deploy, create cloud infrastructure, or write a secret until the responsible owner explicitly approves the concrete provider configuration.
